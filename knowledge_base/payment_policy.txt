PAYMENT POLICY

Supported Payments:
Customers can pay using supported debit cards, credit cards, and other payment methods shown during checkout.

Payment Failure:
If a payment fails, customers should verify their payment information and try the payment again.

Money Deducted:
If money is deducted but the order is not created, the customer should contact support with the transaction details.

Duplicate Payment:
If a customer appears to have been charged more than once for the same order, customer support should investigate the transaction.

Payment Security:
Customers should never share their password, PIN, CVV, or full payment card number with customer support.
