DELIVERY POLICY

Standard Delivery:
Standard delivery usually takes 3 to 5 business days.

Express Delivery:
Express delivery usually takes 1 to 2 business days.

Delivery Tracking:
Customers can track their order using their order ID.

Delayed Delivery:
If an order is delayed beyond the expected delivery date, customers should contact customer support with their order ID.

Delivery Address:
Customers should verify their delivery address before placing an order.

Business Days:
Delivery estimates do not normally include weekends or public holidays.
