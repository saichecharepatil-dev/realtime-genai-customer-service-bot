RETURN AND REFUND POLICY

Return Eligibility:
Customers can request a return within 7 days of receiving the product.

Product Condition:
Products must be unused and returned in their original packaging.

Refund Processing:
After the returned product is inspected and approved, the refund is normally processed within 5 to 7 business days.

Damaged Products:
Customers who receive a damaged product should contact customer support and provide their order ID and evidence of the damage.

Wrong Product:
If the customer receives the wrong product, they should contact customer support with their order ID.

Non-Returnable Products:
Certain products may not be eligible for return according to product-specific policies.
